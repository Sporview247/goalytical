About Goalytical

We explain football matches with numbers.

Every win probability on this site comes from our own model, built on match results. We do not copy or import tips from other websites, and we do not publish betting odds or betting tips.

Projections are estimates, never guarantees. We save every projection before kick-off and score it afterwards, so you can judge us on the record.

Read how the model works. Goalytical is independent and is not affiliated with any league or club.
