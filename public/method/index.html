How our projections work

A results-based goals model. Every number comes from our own calculation: we never copy tips from anyone.

Five steps

1Collect results
Past scores for each league, from football-data.co.uk.
2Rate every team
An attack rating, a defence rating, and one home advantage for the league.
3Weight recent matches
Older games count for less than recent ones, with a half-life of about a year.
4Work out scores
The chance of every scoreline, from 0-0 upward, using a Poisson goals model with a small correction for low-scoring draws.
5Add up outcomes
Home win, draw and away win chances come from adding those scorelines.

Limits

Football has luck in it. A 60% projection still fails four times in ten. The model does not know about injuries, suspensions, lineups or tactics, and it knows little about teams that have just joined a league.

When a team has little recent data in a league, we say so on the match page.

Data and updates

Match results: football-data.co.uk. Fixtures and kick-off times: football-data.org. Projections refresh every six hours and are frozen at kick-off.

We do not use or publish betting odds, and we do not offer betting tips.
